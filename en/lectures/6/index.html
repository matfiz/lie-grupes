Compact Structure and Maximal Tori

What this lecture does

This lecture explains why conjugacy in a compact connected group can be studied on a torus. First, an averaged inner product splits the Lie algebra. Next, maximal tori meet every conjugacy class. Finally, a finite Weyl group records which elements of the torus are conjugate.

Summary

An averaged inner product gives the central and semisimple splitting. Maximal tori are conjugate. For connected KKK, the cited global torus theorem makes exp⁡\expexp surjective. Together with the cited orbit theorem for W(K,T)W(K,T)W(K,T), it identifies the conjugacy classes of KKK with T/W(K,T)T/W(K,T)T/W(K,T). The Weyl group is finite. For U⁡(n)\UU(n)U(n) and SU⁡(n)\SU(n)SU(n) it is isomorphic to SnS_nSn​. Compactness is essential, as the connected group SL⁡(2,R)\SL(2,\R)SL(2,R) shows.
