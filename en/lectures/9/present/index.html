Lecture 9

Weyl integration and character calculations

A class function on a group is a function that is constant on conjugacy classes. On a compact connected group, a class function is determined by its values on a maximal torus. This lecture shows that the Haar integral of a class function can also be computed on the torus. The density is a product over root planes. A factor 1/∣W∣1/|W|1/∣W∣ corrects for repeated conjugates. We then use the formula to check character norms and tensor-product multiplicities.

By the end of this lecture you will be able to:

  • State the Weyl integration formula with its measure conventions and its imported steps.
  • Derive the density det⁡(1−Ad⁡t)\det(1-\Ad_t)det(1−Adt​) on k/t\algk/\algtk/t and the factor 1/∣W∣1/|W|1/∣W∣.
  • Compute class integrals on SU⁡(2)\SU(2)SU(2) and SO⁡(3)\SO(3)SO(3) and compare them.
  • Check character norms and multiplicities with the Weyl character formula on TTT.
