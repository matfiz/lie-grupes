Lecture 17

Unitary induction

Lecture 12 constructed induced representations of compact groups using an invariant measure on K/HK/HK/H. For a noncompact group GGG, the quotient G/HG/HG/H may carry no invariant measure. We replace it by a quasi-invariant measure, which translation by an element of GGG multiplies by a positive continuous function. We then correct the action by a square-root factor. The model is the ax+bax+bax+b group, with representations induced from characters of its translation subgroup.

By the end of this lecture you will be able to:

  • State the quotient-measure theorem and derive the quasi-invariance factor and its cocycle identity.
  • Define the normalised induced representation and prove that it is unitary.
  • Compute the ax+bax+bax+b representations induced from translation characters in two measure pictures.
  • Explain why the unitary equivalence class of the induced representation depends only on the scaling orbit of the frequency.
