Lecture 16

Noncompact Haar Measure and the Modular Function

What this lecture does

Lecture 5 integrated over compact groups. This lecture drops compactness. It asks which translation-invariant integrals a Lie group carries and how left and right integration differ. Every Lie group has a left-invariant and a right-invariant density. With equal normalisations at the identity, the left density divided by the right density is the modular function. It controls right translation, inversion and the right regular representation.
