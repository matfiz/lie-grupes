Lecture 23

Discrete series and the rank one unitary picture

We realise the holomorphic discrete series of SL⁡(2,R)\SL(2,\R)SL(2,R) on weighted spaces of holomorphic functions on the disk. We then compare it with the finite-dimensional and principal series modules. Finally we state the full list of irreducible unitary representations.

By the end of this lecture you will be able to:

  • Prove that Πn\Pi_nΠn​ is an irreducible unitary representation on the weighted disk space An\mathcal A_nAn​ for each integer n≥2n\ge2n≥2.
  • Compute its KKK-types n,n+2,…n,n+2,\dotsn,n+2,… and the action of H′,E,FH',E,FH′,E,F.
  • Decide which lowest weights λ\lambdaλ occur in irreducible unitary representations of SL⁡(2,R)\SL(2,\R)SL(2,R), and when such a representation factors through PSL(2,R)\mathrm{PSL}(2,\R)PSL(2,R).
  • State Bargmann’s classification and derive the positivity bounds behind it.

We import three results from elementary complex analysis. The first is the local power-series theorem. A holomorphic function on a disk has a Taylor series converging uniformly on every smaller closed disk. The second concerns power series. The real Jacobian of a holomorphic map is the squared modulus of its complex derivative. These are the analytic inputs used below. For each integer n≥2n\ge2n≥2, formula (23.5) defines an irreducible unitary representation Πn\Pi_nΠn​ on An\mathcal A_nAn​. Its KKK-types are n,n+2,…n,n+2,\dotsn,n+2,…, each once. The operator EEE raises the KKK-type by 222 and FFF lowers it by 222. The Casimir element Ω\OmegaΩ acts by n(n−2)n(n-2)n(n−2). The relation k2π=Ik_{2\pi}=Ik2π​=I forces integral KKK-types. The representation Πn\Pi_nΠn​ descends to PSL(2,R)\mathrm{PSL}(2,\R)PSL(2,R) exactly for even nnn. In an irreducible unitary module with KKK-types λ,λ+2,…\lambda,\lambda+2,\dotsλ,λ+2,…, the lowest weight is an integer λ≥1\lambda\ge1λ≥1, and Ω\OmegaΩ acts by λ(λ−2)\lambda(\lambda-2)λ(λ−2).
