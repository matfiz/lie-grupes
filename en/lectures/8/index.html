Lecture 8

Highest weights of representations of compact groups

What this lecture does

The Lie algebra course classified finite-dimensional irreducible modules of semisimple algebras by dominant integral weights. This lecture adds the action of the centre of the Lie algebra and decides which of these modules are representations of a given compact connected Lie group KKK. These are exactly the modules whose highest weight is a character of a maximal torus.

Summary

Let KKK be a compact connected Lie group, and assume the cited structure inputs. An irreducible representation of KKK is determined by its highest weight. The possible highest weights are the dominant characters of a maximal torus TTT. For Ksc/FK_{\mathrm{sc}}/FKsc​/F, test FFF on one highest weight vector.
