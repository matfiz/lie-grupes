Lecture 8

Highest weights of representations of compact groups

The Lie Algebras course classified finite-dimensional irreducible modules of semisimple algebras by dominant integral weights. This lecture adds the action of the centre of the Lie algebra and decides which of these modules are representations of a given compact connected Lie group KKK. These are exactly the modules whose highest weight is a character of a maximal torus.

Lecture 7 decided, for a given highest-weight module, whether it descends. It did not show that every irreducible representation of KKK is obtained in this way, or that one is obtained only once. This lecture proves both.

  • Explain why some modules of a reductive Lie algebra come from no compact group.

Summary

Let KKK be a compact connected Lie group, and assume the cited structure inputs. An irreducible representation of KKK is determined by its highest weight.
